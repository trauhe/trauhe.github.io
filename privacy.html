yoga-rclone privacy policy

yoga-rclone runs only on its owner’s own computer. Google Drive data accessed through it is read from and written to the owner’s own Google account only.

No data is collected, stored, shared, sold or sent to any third party or server operated for this app. OAuth tokens are kept only on the owner’s computer, encrypted at rest.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
